State Police

The Pennsylvania State Police formed in 1905, becoming the first uniformed police organization of its kind in the U.S. We provide law enforcement and public safety services to the residents and visitors to the Commonwealth and work alongside our local, state, and federal partner agencies.

This Cadet Candidate Study System provides self-administered examinations and instructional modules for prospective candidates preparing for the Department's selection process. Progress is recorded on this device only.

Study System Overview

The system contains five timed examinations, six instructional modules, a radio code flashcard deck, and physical readiness guidance covering the skill areas assessed during the PSP selection process: observation and memory, judgment and ethics, mathematics and reading comprehension, radio procedure, and Pennsylvania law. Read a module, then run the examination associated with it. Points are recorded to the Candidate Progress Record and determine advancement on the rank schedule, from Trainee to Colonel. Requirements, standards, and statutes change — verify all current requirements with an official recruiter at patrooper.com before applying.

Examinations

Each examination is timed and scored. A personal best is retained for every examination. Explanations follow each question — candidates are directed to review the explanation for every item answered incorrectly. Maximum available: 930 points.

Instructional Modules

Six modules covering the knowledge areas assessed by the selection process and taught at the State Police Academy in Hershey. Mark a module complete after reading to record 25 points.

Study Resources

Supplemental study materials, including the radio code flashcard deck, external video resources, and the recommended daily study schedule.

Radio Code Flashcard Deck

A thirteen-card shuffled deck covering common ten-codes and radio signals, mixed in both directions. Cards are self-graded: five points per card marked correct. Run the full deck daily per the study schedule below.

Physical Readiness

Physical readiness testing is conducted during the selection process. The table below lists commonly cited entry standards. Candidates are advised to begin training well in advance of application and to verify current standards with a recruiter.
